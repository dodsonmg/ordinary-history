Thomas Thatcher's trade shaped the earliest changes to the house. Within a year of buying the property in 1771, he had built a workshop off the rear of the building — the same addition later expanded into the dairy Jane would run for decades.

Parish apprenticeship records show Thomas took on at least two apprentices during the 1780s, suggesting a workshop busy enough to need extra hands. Barrel staves and iron hoop fragments turned up during later renovations are, by local tradition, attributed to this period, though nothing has been formally dated.
