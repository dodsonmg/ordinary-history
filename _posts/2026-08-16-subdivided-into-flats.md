Facing a housing shortage in the surrounding town, the then-owners divided the house into three separate flats in 1932, adding partition walls and a second staircase to the rear extension to give each unit independent access. Rent ledgers from the period show a rotating set of tenant families rather than any single long-term household.

The subdivision lasted until 1946, when Margaret Cole purchased the entire property, removed the partition walls, and reunified it as a single dwelling — the configuration the house has kept since.
