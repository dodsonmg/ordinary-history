Odette Marchetti bought Halloway House in 1975 and spent much of the next eight years treating its grounds as an open-air extension of her studio, installing and rotating pieces from her own concrete and cast-iron work among Fenwick's cantilevered forms. Photographs from this period, widely reprinted in design magazines of the late 1970s, remain some of the most recognizable images of the house.

She made few structural changes to the building itself during her ownership, a fact later conservators would come to appreciate — the house's condition, while already showing signs of wear, did not meaningfully worsen under her care.
