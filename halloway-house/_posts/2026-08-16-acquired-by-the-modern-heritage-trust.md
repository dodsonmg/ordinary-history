After three decades of Gerald Priest's ownership and worsening structural condition, the Modern Heritage Trust acquired Halloway House in 2015 with the explicit goal of stabilizing the building and restoring it as closely as possible to its 1958 appearance and construction. A condition survey commissioned immediately after the purchase found damage significantly more extensive than the visible cracking had suggested, much of it traced back to the 1986 repair campaign rather than to the original 1958 construction.
