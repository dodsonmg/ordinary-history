Gerald Priest bought Halloway House in 1983, aware of its poor condition but confident he could fix it. In 1986 he commissioned a repair campaign intended to stop the concrete's ongoing deterioration — work that, using materials and methods unsuited to the original 1958 construction, ended up trapping moisture inside the structure and accelerating the very damage it was meant to stop. He owned the house for over three decades afterward, largely unable to reverse the problem, before selling to the Modern Heritage Trust in 2015.
