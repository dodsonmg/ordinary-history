The sculptor Odette Marchetti bought Halloway House in 1975, drawn to the raw concrete surfaces she felt suited her own work. She installed a rotating series of her pieces throughout the grounds and, by most accounts, treated the house itself as an extension of her studio practice rather than simply a place to live. Her eight years there, until she sold in 1983, are generally regarded as the house's most photographed period.
