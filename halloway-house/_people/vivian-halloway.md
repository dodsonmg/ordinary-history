Vivian Halloway commissioned the house that bears her name in 1957, giving the young architect Rowan Fenwick unusually free rein over both design and materials. She lived there from its completion in 1958 until 1974, when she sold the property — by her own account, worn down less by the house's severe aesthetic than by the sheer cost of keeping its experimental concrete watertight.
