Sometime around 1815, the single-room workshop behind the house was extended into a larger lean-to structure, enclosing space for the dairy operation Jane Thatcher ran into her sixties. The addition roughly doubled the footprint of the original rear outbuilding.

No building permit survives from this period — the date is inferred from a slight change in brick coursing visible where the extension meets the original rear wall, along with a mention of "the new dairy room" in an 1816 household inventory.
