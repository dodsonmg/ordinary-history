Edward Voss took over the house in 1834 and converted its ground floor into a public house, the Thatcher's Arms — a name that nodded to the family who'd owned the property two generations earlier. Licensing records list him as proprietor until 1861, when he sold the business and moved on; the house continued under other publicans for a few more years before the use changed again.
