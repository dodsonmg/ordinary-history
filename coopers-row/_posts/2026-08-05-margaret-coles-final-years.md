By the early 1970s, Margaret Cole had scaled back the lending library she'd run from the north parlor for over twenty years, though she never formally closed it. Neighbors recalled her still handing out books from the porch well after her retirement from teaching in 1966.

She died in the house in 1978, and by her own instruction the library's remaining shelves were donated to the local primary school rather than kept as a fixture — a detail that later owners have pointed to as the reason no trace of the room's former use survives today.
